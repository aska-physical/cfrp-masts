Plan for passing lattice results into the hardware repos
The lattice proofs and the hardware repositories describe the same truss. A claim moves from one side to the other only when a checker on the receiving side fails a bad case and passes the real case. A model number stays a model number until a bench measurement exists. A new repository waits until a checker produces a result that no current repository can hold.

## What is already on each side

The carbon-fiber truss repository, fibral-rigidity, carries closed forms for the bridged cross-polytope cell. At equal area in three dimensions the bulk modulus is 2(3 + 2√2)/15 of the bound you get when every bar stretches with the volume change and no joint moves. The area ratio √2 times (n − 1) meets that bound for every strut length between 0 and 1.

The uniaxial optimum is 2√6 / 3. SymPy checks those identities for dimensions 2 through 7.

The proof file limits the claim to small-strain pin joints. It says a real joint needs a separate check before a hardware claim.

The organization profile states cooling, carbon, stiffness, and harvested-power figures as product advantages. Those figures are outputs of the product checkers. No bench measurement is attached. The open issue on this repository titled Fuse the two Docks says its rig numbers are presented, not independently checked. graph-fab has a readme and no checker.

===== fibral#4 =====
Two elastic bars join pins a distance 2 apart. A stable shape is an equilibrium whose Hessian is positive definite.

The natural-length box runs from 0.7 to 1.3 and from 0.9 to 1.5. Each of the four corners has a stable shape. The interior pair 0.9 and 1.1 does not. Those lengths sum to the pin span, so the bars lie on one line and the transverse stiffness is zero.

Off that line the bar forces cannot balance. They are not collinear, and circles of radii 0.9 and 1.1 centered 2 apart meet at only the flat point.

A check that looked only at the corners would accept a length pair with no stable shape.

This does not change the bulk-modulus identity of the cross-polytope cell. That identity holds for every strut length once the area ratio is fixed.

The check is `length_box.py`. It passes under `python3` and under `python3 -O`.
